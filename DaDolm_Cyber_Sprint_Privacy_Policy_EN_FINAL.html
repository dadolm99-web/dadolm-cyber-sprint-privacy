Privacy Policy – DaDolm: Cyber Sprint

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Developer and contact

App: DaDolm: Cyber Sprint

2. How the game works

The current version of DaDolm: Cyber Sprint is designed to work offline. It does not offer user accounts, online leaderboards, cloud saves, advertising, or analytics. According to the current app design, the game does not send gameplay or personal data to the developer or to third parties.

3. Data stored locally on your device

The game stores limited game progress locally on your device so that its features work. This may include your score and high score, in-game upgrade levels and currency, selected character, unlocked characters, and music or other game settings where applicable. This information is used only to provide the game's offline functionality and is not transmitted by the game to a server.

Local data remains on the device until it is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. The exact controls for clearing app data may vary by Android device.

4. Personal data collection and sharing

In the current offline version, the game does not ask you to create an account and does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The game does not include advertising or analytics services in this version.

This statement concerns the game itself. Google Play and Android may process information independently when you download, install, update, or run apps, according to their own terms and privacy notices.

5. Permissions and third-party services

The game is intended to run without an internet connection and does not require an online service for its core gameplay. This policy must be updated if a future version introduces advertising, analytics, crash reporting, online features, external SDKs, or permissions that change how information is processed.

6. Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players in the current offline version. The target age group for the Google Play listing must be declared separately and accurately in the Play Console.

7. Your choices and questions

Because game progress is stored locally and the current version does not transmit it to the developer, you can remove that local game data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address provided in Section 1.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app's functions or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when that happens.
